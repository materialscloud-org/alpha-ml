What Shift-ML does

This tool takes an input a crystal structure in Ångström (in a number of different formats), and

  • predicts the NMR chemical shifts of 1H, 13C, 15N and 17O;
  • provides copy-paste content in the extended xyz format and in JSON.

Alternatively, you can calculate an example.

Shift-ML definitions and advantages

The main advantages of this work are:

  • fast and accurate estimation of isotropic NMR chemical shifts.
