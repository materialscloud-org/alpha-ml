What Shift-ML does

This tool takes an input a crystal structure in Ångström (in a number of different formats), and

  • predicts the NMR chemical shifts of 1H, 13C, 15N and 17O;
  • provides copy-paste content in the extended xyz format, in the magres format (the calculated shieldings are given as tensor with the isotropic shielding as diagonal elements) and in JSON.

Alternatively, you can calculate an example.
