What ShiftML does

ShiftML takes as an input a crystal structure (in a number of different formats), and

  • predicts the NMR chemical shifts of 1H, 13C, 15N and 17O;
  • provides copy-paste content in the extended xyz format, in the magres format (the calculated shieldings are given as tensor with the isotropic shielding as diagonal elements) and in JSON.

ShiftML has been tested for DFT-optimized crystal structures of molecular solids containing 1H, 13C, 15N and 17O atoms. Paruzzo, et al. (submitted).
